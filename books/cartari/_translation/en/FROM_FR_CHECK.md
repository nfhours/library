# Ошибки английского перевода, найденные при сверке французского (Opus)

Английский файл не правился. Формат записи: **<глава> блок N** — суть ошибки (итал. → английское → верное).

- proemio, блок 174: Claudian verses, stanza 10, lines 3-4: "All'hor, ch'haurà pietà de' nostri mali / Febo, che questi à modo suo dispensa" — EN swaps the two lines ("when Phoebus, who dispenses them as he wills, / shall have pity on our ills,"), violating line-for-line order; correct: "when there shall have pity on our ills / Phoebus, who dispenses them as he wills,". — **исправлено**
- saturno, блок 76: "ma fà il medesimo di tutto l'anno ancora; perche l'apre quando di Primauera …": the Sun does the same to the whole year (opens it in spring, shuts it in winter), not "through the whole year". EN "does the same through the whole year as well; for he opens it" → "does the same with the whole year as well; for he opens it". — **исправлено**
- diana, блок 112: «ò veramente la Natura delle cose»: «ò veramente» = «or else / or rather» (= ovvero), not an intensifier. EN «or indeed the Nature of things» should read «or else the Nature of things». — **исправлено**
- giunone, блок 28: «Il che dicono de gli altri ancora esser pur troppo vero» = "which others also say is only too true" (de gli altri is partitive: some others say so). EN «And this, as they say of the others too, is only too true» makes it a statement about the others (the other goddesses). Correct: «And this, others too say, is only too true». — **исправлено**
- bacco, блок 146: Italian «Di costui hebbero tanta paura i Dei Celesti, perch'ei si era voltato contra di loro»: the gods feared Typhon BECAUSE he had turned against them (causal «perché»); EN «had such fear of him, when he turned against them» makes it temporal. — **исправлено** («because he had turned against them»)
- venere, блок 99: «le quali, come che fossero forse più de gli altri obligate a questa Dea, riconosceuano da lei quasi tutto…»: «come che» here is causal/qualifying ("as being perhaps more obliged than the others"), which is the reason they acknowledge everything from her; EN «who, although perhaps more bound to this goddess than the others» makes it concessive, which contradicts the logic. — **исправлено** («as being perhaps more bound»)
- gratie, блок 34: Italian «viene ad incontrare Thetide, quando ella và à pregare Volcano, che le voglia dare armi per Achille suo figliuolo» — the mother of Achilles, Thetis; en has «Tethys» (wife of Oceanus), a different goddess. — **исправлено** (Thetide = Thetis, Nereid; глоссарные правки).
- seconda-parte-indiani, блок 27: Italian «due nel mantello, & vna nel corpo dall'Incensiere» (dall' = dell', misprint): the third cross is on the body OF the Censer (the cornucopia); EN «one on the body, by the Censer» places it on the figure's body next to the Censer. — **исправлено** (помета [?3] с пояснением на трёх языках; скан 0604 печатает «dall'», смысл «dell'»; ru/en/fr поправлены: «на теле Кадильницы»).
- annotationi-pignoria, блок 297: L'inscription porte V.S.L.L.M (votum solvit laetus libens merito) ; en « fulfilled his vow willingly and deservedly » omet laetus (« gladly ») (omission mineure). — **исправлено** («gladly, willingly and deservedly»; fr верен)
