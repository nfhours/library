# Ошибки английского перевода, найденные при сверке французского (Opus)

Английский файл не правился. Формат записи: **<глава> блок N** — суть ошибки (итал. → английское → верное).

- proemio, блок 174: Claudian verses, stanza 10, lines 3-4: "All'hor, ch'haurà pietà de' nostri mali / Febo, che questi à modo suo dispensa" — EN swaps the two lines ("when Phoebus, who dispenses them as he wills, / shall have pity on our ills,"), violating line-for-line order; correct: "when there shall have pity on our ills / Phoebus, who dispenses them as he wills,".
- saturno, блок 76: "ma fà il medesimo di tutto l'anno ancora; perche l'apre quando di Primauera …": the Sun does the same to the whole year (opens it in spring, shuts it in winter), not "through the whole year". EN "does the same through the whole year as well; for he opens it" → "does the same with the whole year as well; for he opens it".
- diana, блок 112: «ò veramente la Natura delle cose»: «ò veramente» = «or else / or rather» (= ovvero), not an intensifier. EN «or indeed the Nature of things» should read «or else the Nature of things».
- giunone, блок 28: «Il che dicono de gli altri ancora esser pur troppo vero» = "which others also say is only too true" (de gli altri is partitive: some others say so). EN «And this, as they say of the others too, is only too true» makes it a statement about the others (the other goddesses). Correct: «And this, others too say, is only too true».
- bacco, блок 146: Italian «Di costui hebbero tanta paura i Dei Celesti, perch'ei si era voltato contra di loro»: the gods feared Typhon BECAUSE he had turned against them (causal «perché»); EN «had such fear of him, when he turned against them» makes it temporal.
- venere, блок 99: «le quali, come che fossero forse più de gli altri obligate a questa Dea, riconosceuano da lei quasi tutto…»: «come che» here is causal/qualifying ("as being perhaps more obliged than the others"), which is the reason they acknowledge everything from her; EN «who, although perhaps more bound to this goddess than the others» makes it concessive, which contradicts the logic.
- gratie, блок 34: Italian «viene ad incontrare Thetide, quando ella và à pregare Volcano, che le voglia dare armi per Achille suo figliuolo» — the mother of Achilles, Thetis; en has «Tethys» (wife of Oceanus), a different goddess.
