## Fixes
- *fare* + infinitive / "make" meaning depict: Minos "has him snarl" → "shows him snarling" (17); fables "have him stay … seated" → "represent him … seated" (24); Fulgentius "has him surrounded" → "depicts him" (36); "Hesiod made him with a hundred heads" → "represented him" (49); "Lucian likewise makes him" → "represents him as" (65); Stobaeus "has him boast" → "In Stobaeus … he boasts" (68); "Others have made the Parcae born of Erebus" → "represented … as born" (174); Catullus "making them old of face" → "representing them with old faces" (181); Harpies "in Virgil they once live" → "Virgil once has them living" (116).
- Bellerophon (150): removed the added "but"; *il quale fece sì* is now a plain new sentence "Bellerophon brought it about…" (the Italian *il quale* grammatically follows Proetus, but the sense is Bellerophon).
- Dante/Virgil (199): "as Virgil says to Dante in Dante's poem" → "as in Dante Virgil says to him in this way" (restores *in questa guisa*); *quando però* "when, that is" → "provided, however, that"; *si traheuano* "were drawn" → "made their way".
- *simulacro* = idol: "wooden images" (79), "whose images" (80), "an image made in square form" (186) → idol(s). *Nume saluatico* "wild divinity" → "wild numen" (147).
- E3: "Poets/Philosophers" → lowercase (8 places, as in the reviewed chapters); "furies" → "Furies" in the Dante verse (86); "the Prophet Jeremiah" → "prophet" (140); "Head and leader" → lowercase (190); "Chancellors of the Gods" → "of the gods" (aside 191 and 192); *della Fortuna* (65, the general power, not the Theban cult statue) → "fortune" per the glossary; "death (horrible shapes)" → "Death" in Virgil's list of personifications (218).

- Virgil verse (208): *riuiera* "shore" → "river".
- E1: "meet for it" (archaic) → "fitting for it" (18); "it was observed … to make sacrifice" → "kept the custom of sacrificing" (79); Iasion clause rephrased (68); Statius sentence rebuilt so "showing the joy" refers to Statius, not Tisiphone (100); *dishonesti piaceri* "dishonest" (false friend) → "shameful" (95).

- *Hercole furioso* = *Hercules Furens*; *Gatti Mammoni* = monkeys; *Dio Auerrunco* = Averruncus; *Tone* = Aethon, *Adianto* = maidenhair, *Martiano* = Martianus Capella. All of these follow the glossary.
- "Quasi che altri non fosse…" → "As if there were no one else who could punish him better" is correct, with the hedge kept.
- Notes [?1]–[?4] are well placed and reasonable. The Russian file has no Doubtful section, so the English notes are the translator's own.
- The chapter uses "underworld" for *Inferno* and lowercase "hell" in the allegorical senses ("hell of the vices", "hell of this body/world"). Kept.

## Unresolved / for the editor
