## Doubts left open (for the author)
- 238 (aside) and 239 (text): *Gioue discacciatore di mosche* has the GLOSSARY english form "Zeus Apomyios". The EN uses "Jupiter the Fly-Chaser" (aside) and "the surname Fly-Chaser" (text). Applying the glossary literally ("Jupiter was given the surname Zeus Apomyios") reads badly in a Jupiter context. This is left unchanged for the author to decide: keep the English rendering, or use "Apomyios".
- 73 (Apuleius): *alcune dorate penne poco da quelle differenti, che in forma d'ali spuntauano fuori* is rendered "little different from wings, that stuck out in the shape of wings". This is redundant but faithful to an ambiguous relative, so it is left unchanged.
