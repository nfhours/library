## Translator's doubtful points: verdicts

- "mentre … mi portarà innanzi" → "until": accepted. *Mentre* with the future here means "until / pending the time when" (the offering stands in for a greater service still awaited); "while" would make the two actions simultaneous, which the sense does not support. RU "пока … не принесёт" agrees.
- "per le mie pretensioni" → "for my aspirations": accepted. *Pretensioni* = aims, intentions (here: his wish to repay the favours); "aspirations" is fair and avoids the legal sense of "claims".
- "di mente ancora dell'Autore di esso" → "and I do so also in keeping with the mind of its author": accepted. It qualifies "porgo" (the dedication), as the following "à così fare" confirms; *ancora* = "also" is kept.
- "mostrarmele grato" → "showing myself grateful to you": accepted (mostrar-mi + le, *le* = V.S.).
- "Molt'Illustre" / "Your Worship's": accepted. Address line "Very Illustrious" (glossary-new) and "Your Worship" for V.S. molt'Illustre (GLOSSARY.tsv) keep the distinction from V.S. Illustriss. ("Your Illustrious Lordship"); dropping the repeated adjective in "Di Vostra Sig. molt'Illustre" → "Your Worship's" is correct because "Your Worship" already renders the whole formula.
- Lowercase "treatise", "printing house", "author": accepted (E3; the capitals are typographic). "the author" lowercase matches the convention adopted in aggionta and other EN chapters. "the Images of the Gods" keeps capitals as the name of the part (a title), which is correct.
