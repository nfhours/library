## Title page (scan 0587)

NEW SECOND EDITION
OF THE TRUE AND NEW
IMAGES
OF THE GODS
OF THE INDIANS.

## Dedication (scans 0589–0590)

TO THE VERY ILLUSTRIOUS
my most respected Signor

SIGNOR
PIERO BUARNO [?1].

The memory that I keep of the favours received from Your Worship over a long span of time is so fresh and alive in my mind that, if I had an opportunity at hand as ready for showing myself grateful to you as I know how to wish for one, I would dare to promise myself some lightening of that weight with which your courtesy has loaded me in good measure. Therefore, until some turn of favourable occasion brings me something of greater moment for my aspirations, I offer to Your Worship this treatise, which under the name of the part on the Images of the Gods comes to light from my printing house; and I do so also in keeping with the mind of its author, who holds your worth in singular esteem and has given me a sign that he would be glad if I resolved to do so.

I see it as small in bulk, but I do not esteem it small as regards the opinion that I have heard formed of it by the experts, who hold the author to be a man who has few equals in the knowledge of this kind of antiquity. May Your Worship for now accept my affection, and keep for me your good favour, which I value as much as you know. May Our Lord God grant you prosperity. From Padua, 1 November 1614.

Your Worship's
most humble servant
Pietro Paolo Tozzi.

## Doubtful passages

- [?1] “Piero Buarno” (scan 0589): so in the source, as in the first dedication (scan 0010); no other spelling is found. Rendered “Piero Buarno” [?] (the form of the name is not confirmed).
- “Nova seconda editione” (scan 0587): “New second edition” is as printed; the title opens the Second Part of the book (the gods of the Indies). “Imagini de gli Dei Indiani”: rendered “Images of the Gods of the Indians”.
- “Vere et nove imagini” (scan 0587): *vere* is “true”, *nove* “new”.
- “Molt'Illustre”, “Vostra Sig. molt'Illustre” (scans 0589–0590): a form of address below “Illustrissima”; in the address line the adjective is rendered “Very Illustrious”, and “Vostra Sig. molt'Illustre” is rendered “Your Worship”, as in the glossary (so in the closing “Di Vostra Sig. molt'Illustre” the adjective is not repeated: “Your Worship's”).
- “per le mie pretensioni” (scan 0589): *pretensioni* means claims, aims, designs; rendered “for my aspirations”.
- “Però mentre qualche corso di fauoreuole occasione mi portarà innanzi” (scan 0589): *mentre* is taken here in the sense of “until”, as the sense requires (the offering stands in place of a greater service still to come); rendered “until some turn of favourable occasion brings me”.
- “di mente ancora dell'Autore di esso” (scan 0589): *di mente* means “in accord with the mind, with the knowledge of”; it belongs to “porgo” (the offering), not to the publication: “à così fare” means “to do so”, that is, to dedicate the treatise to Buarno; rendered “and I do so also in keeping with the mind of its author”. The author is not named in the text; left as is.
- “concetto, che ne ho sentito a formare da gl'intendenti” (scan 0590): *concetto* means “conception, opinion”; rendered “the opinion that I have heard formed of it by the experts”.
- “Il Nostro Sig. Iddio” (scan 0590): “Our Lord God”, the usual pious formula.
- The date “1 November 1614” is printed in the 1626 edition; the text does not explain the discrepancy with the year of the edition; the date is given as printed.
- The old Italian spellings “riceuuti”, “osseruandiss.”, “fauori”, “cosi”, “rissol-/uesse” (u = v; word carried over the page) are silently normalised.
- There are no printer's errors affecting the sense on these scans.
