# Review: catalogo-dei (EN)

Checked entry by entry against pages 0017–0022 and ru/catalogo-dei.md. Block structure unchanged; `build_chapters.py --lang en --check catalogo-dei` prints `catalogo-dei: OK`.

## English fixes

- Block 7 (item 5): "the great universal Mother" became "the universal Great Mother". The glossary has *gran Madre* = Great Mother, and the old wording mixed the capitals.
- Block 9 (item 7): "presiding over hospitality and helping others" became "presiding over hospitality and over giving aid". *sopra … il giouare* names a domain. "Others" was added, and the old wording read as if Jupiter himself helps.
- Block 42 (items 41–43): "bitterly punishing" became "harshly punishing". *aspramente* means harshly.
- Block 50 (item 51): "goddess bringer of" became "goddess, bringer of". The missing comma made the phrase hard to read.
- Block 62 (item 63): "like the gods the Lares" became "like the Lares gods". *come li dei Lari*; the old wording was unidiomatic.
- Block 76 (item 77): "peace and civility" became "peace and of civil life". *ciuiltà* means civic, civil life, not politeness.
- Block 80 (item 81): "brother of Amor" became "brother of Cupid". Glossary rule 6 gives *Amore* = "Cupid (Amor)", so the first form is used. The cupido chapter never uses "Amor".
- Doubtful passages, last item: added a note on the Cupid/Amor choice (items 80–81) and the Uranus/Heaven choice (item 99).

## Rulings on the translator's flagged points

- Item 60 [?2]: "know [?2]" is kept. *fanno* makes no sense here, and the Janus chapter (*sapeua*) supports *sanno*. The mark is in the right place.
- Item 65: "Fortune" with a capital is correct by E3 (goddess, personification). The glossary's lowercase *fortune* is for the general power.
- Item 80: "Cupid, or Amor" is kept. Both names are needed, and "Amor" is the glossary's own alternative form. Item 81 is now "Cupid" (see above).
- Item 18: "Tethys" is correct. The glossary maps *Thetide/Tetide* to Tethys, and *Tethide* is a variant spelling. Kept by rule 8.
- Item 99: "to Uranus, or to Heaven" is kept. Uranus matches the glossary (*Urano*). *Cielo* is capitalized as the alternative name of the same god, which is also the last sphere in the planetary sequence. Item 4, "god of heaven", stays lowercase.
- *orti* = "kitchen gardens", *giardini* = "gardens": correct and consistent in items 27–29.
- Item "190" Calliope: the misprinted number is kept as printed and explained in the notes. The translation itself is correct.
- The other misprinted numbers (21 for 22, 14 for 24) are kept as printed. [?1] and [?2] are intact.
