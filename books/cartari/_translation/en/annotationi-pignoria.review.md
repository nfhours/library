# Review: annotationi-pignoria (EN)

I compared all 390 blocks with the Italian (TEXT_reading.md, scans 0503–0570) and the Russian. Block structure is unchanged, and `build_chapters.py --lang en --check annotationi-pignoria` prints `annotationi-pignoria: OK`. All [?1]–[?21] marks are still in place.

## English fixes (block: what / why)

Sense, omissions and wording
1. 47: "Bromius the Father" changed to "Bromius, the Father". Pater is a separate Mithraic grade in Jerome's list, and the Russian separates it too.
2. 49: "which I do not know by whom was explained" was ungrammatical. It now reads "which someone, I do not know who, has explained".
3. 111: "fin'hora" was left out. Added "up to now".
4. 125: "a carte 31" changed to "fol. 31" (rule car./carte = fol.). The draft had "page 31".
5. 127: "del già Franc." changed to "son of the late Francesco". The draft's "of the late Francesco" was unclear.
6. 129: "vanità" is now "an empty fancy". "a vanity" is a false friend.
7. 145: "secondo che i Poeti" is now "as the poets give her" (not causal "since").
8. 149: "O i Poeti le Chiamano Dracones. è vero" is an anticipated objection, so "Oh," became "Yet the poets call them *Dracones*. That is true; …". **Ruling:** objection, not interjection.
9. 209: "internarsi in lui" is now "venture far into him". "go inland" meant the opposite.
10. 231: "vel magis miranda" is now "even more to be wondered at". "or even" mistranslated *vel*.
11. 255: "in habito poco meno, che di Mercurio" is now "in a garb almost that of Mercury".
12. 277: the hedge "(come si dice)" was missing. Added "as they say".
13. 287: "se però … non hebbe pensiero di alludere" means "unless". The English fragment "If, however, he … did not have it in mind" is now "; unless, however, whoever had it struck intended to allude to the conceit of Seneca …".
14. 294: "valente" is now "skilled" ("valiant" is a false friend).
15. 312: "a capitoli due" is now "at chapter 2" (cf. block 96: Casaubon, Book 1, ch. 2). The draft had "in two chapters". Also "Book" changed to "book".
16. 324: ΘΕΩ "To God" is now "To the god". It is a pagan dative, so lowercase (E3). "forfanterie" is now "knaveries" ("bravadoes" was wrong).
17. 344: "Nemesi de i Tianei" is now "the Nemesis of the people of Tyana", consistent with "Apollonius of Tyana" and glossary Tiana = Tyana. **Ruling** on "Tianei". I updated the glossary-new row to match.
18. 346 (Fol. 380, garbled): "In the hand of Justice is this a bundle …" was broken English. It now reads "In the hand of Justice is this bundle of those rods that the lictors anciently carried before the magistrates, taken from an ancient sepulchre". "a knot of figures very graceful" is now "a very graceful knot of figures". **Ruling:** I translated it without conjecturing a missing participle (see questions).
19. 348: "valiant painter" is now "skilled painter".
20. 352: "io dubito che si sia equiuocato" means "I suspect he was mistaken". "I doubt that there has been some mistake" said the opposite. It now reads "I suspect that the Author has been mistaken in some way".
21. 369: "fatture Magiche" is now "magic charms". *Fattura* here is a spell-object, so "magical works" was too literal. **Ruling.**

Glossary and name consistency
22. 115: "Fornuto" is now "Cornutus". The glossary's first form is Cornutus (Phurnutus); the draft had "Phornutus".
23. 244: "Monsig. Patriarca Grimani" is now "Monsignor the Patriarch Grimani". The draft had "Most Reverend", which belongs to "Reverendiss.".
24. 253: "GIO. GIORGIO HERWART" is now "JOHANN GEORG HERWART", the same form as block 143. "a Book" is now "a book".
25. 329: "EDMOND BRUTZ" is now "EDMUND BRUTZ", the form used in the glossary-new proposal.
26. 352: "Chero o Cero" is now "The god Kairos, or Cero". The glossary English is "Kairos (Chero)" and the bracket stays out of the text (rule 6), as in *fortuna.md*. **Ruling:** Kairos, not Cero. The Doubtful-passages note is updated.
27. 379: "l'Amorino" is now "the Amorino". The glossary has Amorini, as used in block 375; the draft had "little Cupid".
28. 383: "IOCVS" is now "JOCUS". The glossary entry is Gioco = Jocus, "IOCVS". **Ruling:** the draft's "JEST" goes, and the Russian «Игра» follows the Russian glossary, so it is not a Russian error. Also "Apian" at Fol. 439 stays. **Ruling:** a gem drawing points to Peter Apian (glossary "Pietro Appiano" = Peter Apian, as at Fol. 71, block 57), not the historian Appian.
29. 385: "Venus Kallipyge (*Callipiga*)" is now "Venus Kallipyge". The glossary English is Kallipyge; the bracket came from the comment column.
30–33. 141, 152, 160, 203: "the author" (Cartari) is now "the Author", consistent with the rest of the chapter.

60. Doubtful passages: the "Cedolone"/"da in zara" note quoted renderings that are not in the text. It now matches the text ("a sort of large schedule", "let him who gambles bear his own loss").

Points accepted as they were (rulings)
- [?5] Macrobius "mouths pressed together": accepted. The printed *oculis* is corrupt for *osculis*.
- [?6] "Livinus Torrentius": accepted. Block 36 has *Leuino Torrentio*, so the correction is consistent.
- "Hasta pura" as "a plain spear": accepted (it means a spear without an iron head).
- "Tauola Itineraria" as "Itinerary Table": accepted (it is the Peutinger Table).
- "Bragada": kept as printed, with the note.
- "Apian" at Fol. 439: kept (see 28).

## Russian errors (vs Italian; not corrected, also in .ru-errors.tsv)
- Block 166: the text has «Ленином Торренцием», but note [?6] says the name was corrected to «Левин». It should be «Левином Торренцием» (cf. block 36).
- Block 281: «Оккона, на которого ссылается Джиральди» is wrong. "l'Ocone, che cita il Giraldi" means Occo, who cites Giraldi.
- Block 287: the sense is inverted («не думал намекать»). The Italian means "unless he intended to allude to Seneca".
- Block 312: «в двух главах» is wrong. "a capitoli due" means chapter 2.

## Open questions for the author
1. Fol. 380 (block 346): "si è questo vn fascio": "posto" may be missing ("there has been put this bundle"). I rendered it neutrally. Should "posto" be supplied?
2. Block 181: "i Fondachi hora non ci stanno per nulla" was rendered "the warehouses now would not hold it at all". It might instead mean "today's cloth-shops are nothing beside it". Please check.
3. "Goffredo Liuocerio" (block 40, probably Linocier) is kept in the Italian form and has no glossary-new row. Should it be Latinised (Godefridus Linocerius)?
4. Hybrid forms "Gio. Vivianus", "Gio. Vicenzo della Porta", "Gio. Mario Mattio": keep the Italian "Gio." abbreviation, or expand it?
5. Block 132: Macrobius' *dracones* is rendered "serpents", while block 150 stresses that Macrobius calls them *Dracones*. Should it be "dragons" in the quote?
