# Review: The Graces (en/gratie.md, scans 0493–0501)

Checked block by block against `_text/pages/0493–0501.md` (text, captions, marginalia), then against `ru/gratie.md`. Structure check: `gratie: OK` (47 blocks; verse 14 + 21 lines, as in the Italian and the Russian; three captions; asides in the Russian positions). Glossary forms checked (Horae, Graces, Parcae, Tethys for *Thetide*, Sleep, Cytherea, Eunomia, Eteocles, Alexander ab Alexandro, Eleans, Colonna): all correct.

## Fixes
- Block 3: *Alcuni altri le fanno essere nate in altro modo* — "Others have them born" (fare-calque, E5.2/5.3) → "Others represent them as born in another way".
- Block 13: Eusebius' baskets: "Spring … Summer" → "spring … summer" (E3: seasons, not personified here).
- Block 27: *maggiore assai: & molte volte duplicato* — "many times doubled" (reads as "multiplied many times over") → "often doubled".
- Block 46, verse lines 5–6: *Tutte tre son d'età pari, & bellezza / Pur'anco pari in tutte tre si vede* — the subject of line 6 is *bellezza* ("and beauty / is likewise seen equal in all three"); the English had "equal in age and beauty, / and equality is likewise seen in all three" (redundant, with *pari* turned into a noun). Now: "all three are equal in age, and beauty / is likewise seen equal in all three."
- Block 46, verse line 9: *Con le loro, che sono a la sinistra, / Et a la destra risguardando a noi* — the sisters stand on the left and on the right, looking at us (Thalia, with her back to us, links arms with them). "with theirs, which are on the left," (arms on the left) → "with theirs, who are on the left" (the line now runs on into "and on the right, looking at us,").
- Doubtful passages, unnumbered note on *farsi bene*: *al farti bene* is on scan 0500, not 0498. Corrected.
- Doubtful passages [?2]: added the alternative comparative reading of *così è mal … [come]* (see below). The translation itself is unchanged.

## Translator's flagged points
- *Benche Pausania scriue* → "Pausanias, though, writes": accepted. The concessive is kept and no fragment is left. The Russian does NOT drop it: «Правда, Павсаний пишет» is the same solution.
- *Perche queste dunque fanno … fu detto … perche tante sono*: "Since … it was said … because" is correct. The two causal clauses are kept and distinguished. The Russian splits the sentence («Эти Оры — причина… Отсюда и говорилось»), which is fine.
- *Onde le nominiamo hora* → "So we now name them": accepted. *nominiamo* has the same form for indicative and 1st-person-plural imperative, so the Russian «назовём» is also a valid reading. Not an error.
- *facendo che fosse loro scorta & duce Mercurio* → "depicting Mercury as their escort and leader": correct (E5.3, the image).
- *delle gratie sue* → "its favours": correct. The pun on Graces cannot be kept, and E5.5 does not apply because this is not an etymology.
- *Aristotile nelle Morali* → *Moral Writings*: accepted per rule 8. FYI: the passage is Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* V.5 (1133a), the temple of the Charites set up in a conspicuous place. Nothing added to the text.
- [?1] *Oltre di ciò mette, Ethocle … fosse il primo*: "he states [?1] that …" is correct. The mark sits at *mette*, as in the Russian («он сообщает [?1], что»).
- [?2] *così è mal dare a chi non merita, ò non ne hà cui fa dibisogno*: "rather than" is supplied, and the Russian does the same («а не тому, кому…»). This does not distort the meaning. The resulting sense (do not give to the undeserving or unneedy instead of the needy and deserving) matches the caption on scan 0499 (*à meriteuoli*). However, *così … [come]* may be a lost comparison: "it is as bad to give to the undeserving as [to fail to give] to the deserving". I added this to the note. The mark is in the same place as in the Russian (end of the sentence).
- Colonna verse *Pur'anco pari in tutte tre si vede*: fixed (see Fixes). The subject is *bellezza*, carried over from line 5.
- *alma Citherea* → "gracious Cytherea": acceptable (*alma* = nurturing, kindly; «благодатной» in the Russian). "bountiful" would be a closer alternative. Left to the author.
- *le fanno essere quattro* → "they are made out to be four": acceptable. It means "are held to be", and no image is involved, so this is not a fare = depict case.
- [?N] marks: the text has [?1] (block 32) and [?2] (block 39), the same as the Russian. The Doubtful section has entries for both, plus unnumbered notes. They match.
- THE END. = «КОНЕЦ.», correct.

## RU section
- Block 46 (Colonna verse, lines 9–11): the Russian «с их руками, что слева, / а справа, глядя на нас, / эта носит имя Эвфросина, та — Аглая» puts the arms on the left and only one figure on the right. In the Italian, *che sono a la sinistra, / Et a la destra risguardando a noi* refers to the two sisters, who stand left and right and look at us. Listed in gratie.ru-errors.tsv.
- Not counted (no change of meaning): line 6 «и равными видны все три» misses that the subject is *bellezza*, but the sense (equal beauty) survives; block 34 «Другую он называет Грацией» for *ne chiama ancora vna* ("also calls one of them").
