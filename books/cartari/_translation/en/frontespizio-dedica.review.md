## Fixes

1. Block 4: "SECOND NEWEST EDITION" → "NEWEST SECOND EDITION". It. "SECONDA NOVISSIMA EDITIONE" = the second edition, the newest one. In English "second newest" means "the one before the newest" (penultimate), which reverses the sense. The new order also matches the sister title "NOVA SECONDA EDITIONE" → "NEW SECOND EDITION" (seconda-parte-titolo-dedica, block 2).
2. Block 9: "With the Allegories on the Images by CESARE MALFATTI" → "With the Allegories on the Images, by CESARE MALFATTI". Without the comma the English reads "Images by Malfatti". It. "Allegorie sopra le Imagini di Cesare Malfatti": Malfatti is the author of the Allegories (GLOSSARY.tsv; "migliorate, & accresciute" agrees with *Allegorie*), as RU "С Аллегориями ЧЕЗАРЕ МАЛЬФАТТИ … на Образы" has it.
3. Block 15 (E1): the second sentence (about 95 words) is split after "than the name of Your Illustrious Lordship." The relative "che sò gradirà" (subject V.S.) becomes "I know that you will welcome…". No word is lost or added.

The relative "fauori, che non si possono…" becomes "Because of their greatness, these favours can only be pointed out…", with the subject named explicitly (E4). ## Translator's doubtful points: verdicts

- "professa tanto quanto egli è, tutto hauere, & riconoscere in gran parte dalla humanità": accepted. Both infinitives depend on *professa* and share *dalla humanità* ("riconoscere X da Y" = acknowledge owing X to Y), and *in gran parte* goes with *riconoscere*. The slight illogic (he *has* everything from her but *acknowledges* it "in large part") is in the original. "who professes that everything he is, he has from the kindness of Your Illustrious Lordship, and largely acknowledges it" is faithful. RU agrees.
- "si per dar loro nome, come apunto mi riuscì" → "to make a name for them, as I indeed managed to do": accepted. *Dar nome* = give renown. The pun with "col nome di V.S." ("the name of") survives in English, and *come apunto mi riuscì* is kept.
- "Colendissimo" → "most honoured": accepted. It stays distinct from "osseruandiss." → "most respected" in the sister dedication. (The RU column of glossary-new had a typo, "мнопочтеннейший"; I corrected it to "многопочтеннейший", as in the RU file. The English form is unchanged.)
- "SECONDA NOVISSIMA EDITIONE": the translator's "SECOND NEWEST EDITION" was wrong in English (see Fix 1). It is now "NEWEST SECOND EDITION". RU "ВТОРОЕ НОВЕЙШЕЕ ИЗДАНИЕ" is correct in Russian.
- "Ridotte da capo a piedi alle loro reali, & non più per l'adietro osseruate simiglianze" → "Brought back from head to foot to their true likenesses, not observed before": accepted. *Ridurre a* = bring into (a form); "brought back" is defensible because the likenesses are those of the ancient originals. "From head to foot" keeps the image (the figures are corrected completely), and *non più per l'adietro* = "not before" is kept.
- "che sò gradirà" → "I know … will welcome": accepted. The subject is V.S., not *nome*, and *sò* is kept as "I know" (now the main clause after Fix 3).
- "le ha legate insieme" → "has bound you together": accepted. The feminine plural *le* refers to the two persons, addressed as Signorie (V.S. and Signor Buarno), and *degl'animi* (minds) rules out "the two parts of the book". RU "связало вас между собой" agrees.
- "Allegorie sopra le Imagini di Cesare Malfatti": Malfatti is the author of the Allegories. The construction was ambiguous in English and is fixed with a comma (Fix 2).

- "the Images" / "Cartari's Images of the Gods" keep their capital as the title of the work, which is correct.
